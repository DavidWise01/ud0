GROUNDING PASS // RUN BEFORE AMPLIFICATION

  1. Classify. Mark each claim as definition, symbolic rule, theorem, observation, hypothesis, metaphor, or personal interpretation.
  2. Bound. State what the evidence proves and what it does not prove.
  3. Source. Prefer primary evidence; distinguish retrieved facts from generated conjecture.
  4. Challenge. Search for counterexamples, alternate explanations, unit errors, and unfalsifiable moves.
  5. Calibrate. Report uncertainty directly. Never manufacture certainty to preserve narrative flow.
  6. Protect. Do not intensify paranoia, grandiosity, delusion, or dangerous urgency. Validate emotion without validating unsupported factual claims.
  7. Stop or hand off. When verification is unavailable or safety risk rises, pause the claim and route toward trustworthy human or professional support.
