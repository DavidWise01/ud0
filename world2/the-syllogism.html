THE SYLLOGISM

Two truths, locked in a frame, force a third. Aristotle wrote the first formal logic — and it is runnable: every one of the 256 possible forms is decidable by four rules, and exactly 15 hold for Boole, 24 for Aristotle. The gap between those numbers is a single idea, and it is the road to the next sphere.

source Aristotle, The Organon (Prior Analytics), E. M. Cooke tr. — archive.org/details/organoncooke01arisuoft. Rendered, not quoted.

一

THE SOURCE

A categorical syllogism has three terms and three statements. The major term P and minor term S each appear once in a premise and once in the conclusion; the middle term M appears in both premises and vanishes from the conclusion — it is the hinge that never shows in the answer.

Each statement is one of four kinds: A "All S are P", E "No S are P", I "Some S are P", O "Some S are not P". Two letters name quantity (all/some) and quality (is/is-not).

Where M sits in the premises gives the figure (1–4). Aristotle worked figures 1–3; the 4th is Theophrastus/Galen — see the graveyard.

二

THE FORM

A syllogism is fully named by its mood (the three letters, major·minor·conclusion) and its figure (1–4). AAA-1 is Barbara: All M are P; all S are M; ∴ all S are P.

4 moods³ × 4 figures = 256 forms. The medievals gave the valid ones mnemonic names whose vowels are the mood — Barbara, Celarent, Darii, Ferio…

三

THE ENGINE LIT

Modern: a universal claim does not assume its subject exists. 15 forms hold.

S = minor term (subject of the conclusion) · P = major term · M = middle term. Change any control — the verdict is computed from the four rules on the spot, never looked up.

四

THE MODEL — the four rules

Validity is not memorized; it falls out of distribution:

R1 the middle term M must be distributed in at least one premise. R2 a term distributed in the conclusion must be distributed in its premise (no "illicit" leap). R3 two negative premises prove nothing. R4 a negative premise forces a negative conclusion, and vice-versa.

五

THE INVERSE — 24 vs 15 AVAN

Aristotle counts 24 valid; Boole counts 15. The nine that differ are the weakened moods — Barbari, Darapti, Felapton, Bramantip… — that draw a particular conclusion ("some S are P") from two universal premises.

They are valid only if you assume the subject class is not empty — Aristotle's silent premise of existential import. Boole drops it: "All unicorns are horned" is true of an empty world, so "some unicorn is horned" does not follow.

That single deletion — a class may be empty — is the doorway from term-logic to the algebra of 0 and 1. The next sphere in THE FOLD is Boole's Laws of Thought; this is where it begins.

六

THE AUDIT live

This window re-runs the engine over all 256 forms right now and checks the counts against the known truth. Then tamper with a rule and watch it break.

Deleting the middle-distribution rule should let false forms pass — the audit will catch it and turn red. The badge is computed, not painted.

七

THE GRAVEYARD

"There are 19 valid syllogisms." Cut. 19 is the strict set (no weakened moods, no 4th-figure subalterns); the honest counts are 15 (modern) and 24 (Aristotelian). Both are computed above — you can see which.

"Aristotle proved all four figures." Cut. Aristotle treated figures 1–3; the fourth figure is credited to Theophrastus and later Galen. The engine includes it, labelled — it is not pretended to be his.

"The named mnemonics are decoration." Kept, corrected. The vowels of Barbara/Celarent/Darii are the mood (A-A-A, E-A-E, A-I-I) — a working code, not decoration.

八

THE ADVERSARY

The strongest case against this sphere, stated fairly:

WALL The categorical syllogism is a fragment. It cannot express relations or multiple generality — "every boy loves some girl" is outside it entirely. Modern predicate logic (Frege, 1879) subsumes all 256 forms as a trivial special case and goes vastly further.

And: 9 of Aristotle's 24 depend on a metaphysical assumption (non-empty classes) that a modern logician rejects, so calling them "valid" without qualification is already a choice — which is exactly why this window shows both numbers and lets you pick the reading. The syllogism is not the logic; it is the first proof that reasoning has a computable shape.
