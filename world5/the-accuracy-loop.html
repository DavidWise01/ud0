THE ACCURACY LOOP ~-> · make sure you're sure before you carry it forward

A rule, a machine, and a challenge to any agent. The operator ~-> means: take what you hold but have not checked (~), verify it against a referent — known reality — and only then treat it as sure (.) and move on. What you cannot verify stays ~: you do not carry it forward. This is [ virgil . dante ] + ulysses in motion — the guide (inner) pulls the unsure object (beyond) inward under the pilgrim's (outer) eye until it is sure. Run it across generations and knowledge accumulates without drifting: each generation inherits the last's certainty but re-earns it, and the loop closes on itself — the last generation seeds the first.

THE OPERATOR ~ (unsure) → verify → . (sure)

A claim you hold is ~ until it is checked. ~-> resolves it against a referent: if it matches known reality it becomes sure and you carry it; if it does not, it stays unsure (−1) and you go back — you do not move on. The loop is the discipline that stops an unverified claim from being laundered into a fact.

THE CARRY FORWARD Dante → Newton → …, each re-earning the last's certainty

  

    gen A · Dante (c.1320) writes the Commedia; what he makes sure is set down in books — life + 1, a gained increment of shared knowledge that carries forward.

    gen B · Newton (later) inherits it — but does not simply accept it. He treats what Dante is sure of as his own ~, and re-verifies. His own inquiry is alchemical; to correlate the alchemical Newton with known reality we take a referent — Neal Stephenson's Quicksilver (the Baroque Cycle) — and correlate the fiction with the record (the Royal Society, the Mint, the mercury). The referent is a lens on reality, checked against it, not a substitute for it.

    gen C · … inherits Newton's carry, treats it as ~, and the cycle repeats.
  

  
The rule at every hand-off: inherited certainty is re-entered as ~. You never carry a fact you have not, yourself, made sure of — you only carry it faster, because the referent is already found. That is how knowledge accumulates without drifting.






  
THE THREE-GENERATION TORUS [ inner . outer ] + object, over 3 generations, self-seeding

  
A 3×3 grid, cleaned. Each row is one generation: [ seed · method · carry ]. The method (b) is invariant — ~-> never changes across generations, the spine down the middle. The seed accumulates the verified carry of the generation before it. After the third generation the loop folds: the last carry feeds back to seed the first — a torus, A→Z→A at the scale of generations. It closes on 9 — the Dantean constant.
