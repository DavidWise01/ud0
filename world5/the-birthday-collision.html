THE BIRTHDAY COLLISION 23 people, even odds

Why hash tables collide sooner than you expect: with m slots you get a 50% collision after only about 1.2√m insertions, not m/2. The classic case: 23 people share a birthday with probability just over ½. Here i-13 multiplies the no-collision odds the product (m−i)/m for 23 in 365 and gets P(collision) = 0.5073.
