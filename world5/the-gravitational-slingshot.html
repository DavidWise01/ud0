THE GRAVITATIONAL SLINGSHOT steal a planet's speed

A free ride across the solar system: fly behind a moving planet, and in the Sun’s frame you leave with up to twice the planet’s velocity added — an elastic bounce off a mass so huge it barely notices. The planet loses an unmeasurable sliver of orbital speed; you gain kilometers per second. Voyager reached the outer planets on Jupiter’s slingshot; it is momentum, borrowed.

THE TECHNIQUE v_out = v_in + 2U (head-on flyby)

The demo runs a head-on elastic flyby: incoming 10 km/s off a planet moving 13 km/s: live demo







  
HISTORY & CREDIT Minovitch · 1961

  
“A slingshot creates energy.” — energy is conserved; the spacecraft borrows kinetic energy from the planet’s orbit, which slows by an immeasurable amount. cited
