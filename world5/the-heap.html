THE HEAP the priority queue in an array — insert by sifting up

A binary heap is the workhorse priority queue: it keeps the maximum (or minimum) instantly available at the root, with O(log n) insert and extract. Like heapsort’s (dart 505) it lives in a flat array by index, but its other motion is sift-up: to insert, place the new element at the end and swap it upward with its parent while it is larger, until the heap order is restored. It is how Dijkstra’s shortest paths, Huffman coding, event simulations, and task schedulers pick “what next” efficiently. Insert 3,1,4,1,5 one at a time and the root holds the maximum, 5.

THE TECHNIQUE insert at the end, sift up past smaller parents — O(log n)
