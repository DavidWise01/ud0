THE SCHWARZSCHILD RADIUS the edge of the void

Squeeze any mass inside rₛ = 2GM/c² and escape velocity reaches the speed of light: it becomes a black hole, and that radius is its event horizon — the edge past which the void keeps everything, even light. Earth’s is 8.87 mm; the Sun’s is 2.95 km. Karl Schwarzschild solved Einstein’s equations for it in 1916, from a trench on the Eastern Front.
