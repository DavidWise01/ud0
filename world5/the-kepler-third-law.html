THE KEPLER THIRD LAW the period squared is the size cubed

For every body orbiting the same mass, T² ∝ a³ — the period squared is proportional to the semi-major axis cubed, with the same constant for all of them. Kepler found it in Tycho’s data in 1619, decades before Newton explained it as T² = 4π²a³/GM. The ratio T²/a³ is a fingerprint of the central mass.
