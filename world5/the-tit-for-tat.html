THE TIT-FOR-TAT cooperate first, then echo

Four lines of code that won a tournament and reframed how we think about cooperation: tit-for-tat cooperates on the first move, then copies whatever the opponent did last. In Axelrod’s iterated prisoner’s dilemma tournaments it beat every cleverer strategy. Its virtues: nice (never defects first), retaliating (punishes at once), forgiving (returns to cooperation), and clear (easy to read). It never wins a single match — it just never loses badly.
