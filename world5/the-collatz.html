COLLATZ halve it or triple-plus-one — everything, so far, falls into 4→2→1

Pick any positive integer. If it is even, halve it; if odd, triple it and add one. Repeat. However wildly the number bounces — 27 climbs past nine thousand before it comes down — it has always, so far, fallen into the fixed cycle 4 → 2 → 1 → 4. That the sequence always reaches 1 is the Collatz conjecture: checked to 2⁷⁰ and beyond, proven for none. It is a fixed point (a cycle, really) that the whole number line seems to drain into, by a rule a child can follow and no one can crack. Erdős: “Mathematics is not yet ready for such problems.”
