THE MICHAELIS-MENTEN how fast an enzyme works

The founding equation of enzyme kinetics: reaction velocity v = Vmax[S] / (Km + [S]). At low substrate the enzyme races linearly; as substrate floods in, every active site fills and the rate saturates at Vmax. The constant Km is the substrate level at which the enzyme runs at half speed — a measure of its grip. Published in 1913 by Leonor Michaelis and Maud Menten, one of the first women to earn a medical doctorate in Canada.
