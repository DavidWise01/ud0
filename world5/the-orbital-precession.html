THE ORBITAL PRECESSION the ellipse that slowly turns

A perfect 1/r² orbit closes into a fixed ellipse. Real orbits precess — the ellipse itself slowly rotates. Mercury’s perihelion advances 43 arcseconds per century that Newton could not explain; general relativity supplies exactly that, 6πGM / c²a(1−e²) per orbit. It was Einstein’s first triumph — a known anomaly, predicted to the arcsecond.
