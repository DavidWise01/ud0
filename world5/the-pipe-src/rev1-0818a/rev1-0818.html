W3 · 1D · THE LADDER METAL

W4 · 2D · THE COMMUTATOR FIRE


Plain English: the interactive. Pick a surface move and a burrow depth. It runs both orders — move-then-burrow and burrow-then-move — and shows the difference. Zero means they don't interfere (orthogonal). Flip the TAMPER switch to inject a broken "spilling" burrow that MUST make the difference non-zero — that's how you know the instrument can actually see a fault.
