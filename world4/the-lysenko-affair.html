1

ROOTS — the state takes biology 1948

Trofim Lysenko rose under Stalin promising fast harvests through vernalization — chilling seed to change its growth. He rejected the gene and the chromosome as “bourgeois” invention, teaching instead that traits acquired in a plant’s lifetime pass to its offspring.

At the August 1948 VASKhNIL session (the Lenin All-Union Academy of Agricultural Sciences), Mendelian genetics was declared false and effectively banned in Soviet science and teaching. Dissent was career-ending, and worse.

2

MENDEL WORKS — segregation LIT

Each parent carries two alleles per gene and passes one at random (Mendel’s law of segregation). Cross two heterozygotes Aa × Aa and the offspring genotypes fall 1 : 2 : 1 (AA : Aa : aa); with A dominant, the visible phenotypes fall 3 : 1.

Two independent genes AaBb × AaBb give the classic 9 : 3 : 3 : 1. These are not opinions — they are counted in gardens and they reproduce. The engine to the right runs the square and checks it.

HARDY–WEINBERG — equilibrium LIT

For an allele at frequency p (and q = 1−p), a large random-mating population settles at genotype frequencies

p² + 2pq + q² = 1

Anchor p = 0.6:   0.36 (AA) + 0.48 (Aa) + 0.16 (aa) = 1. The identity is algebraic — it holds for every p, and the engine samples many to prove it sums to one.

THE COST — what the ban cost grave

WALL Nikolai Vavilov — the world’s foremost plant geneticist, founder of the great seed bank in Leningrad — was arrested in 1940 and died of starvation in prison in 1943, while Lysenko ran Soviet biology into the 1960s.

An estimated ~3,000 biologists were dismissed, imprisoned, or worse. Genetics teaching was gutted; Soviet agriculture and biology were set back a generation. These are cited historical facts, recorded here without embellishment.

7

THE GRAVEYARD — the dead claim

“Acquired characteristics are heritably transmitted.” DEAD. As a general mechanism of inheritance it has no carrier and no reproducible result. A cold-treated seed does not pass its chilling to its offspring’s genome; the trait reverts. The claim fails the cross.

precise, not gloating Epigenetics is real — and limited. Some marks (e.g. methylation) can be inherited across a few generations. That is a narrow, mechanistic phenomenon; it is not a vindication of Lysenko, who denied the gene itself and promised heritable transformation on demand. Naming it here only to pre-empt the myth.
8

THE TAMPER — accept Lysenkoism

The red move: adopt Lysenko’s rule inside the engine — deny segregation, let each parent transmit its expressed character wholesale (the acquired/dominant trait), not a randomly segregated allele.

Under Lysenkoism the Punnett square collapses: the recessive allele “disappears,” the cross predicts 4 : 0 instead of 3 : 1, and the witness (5) — comparing the prediction to what gardens actually count — turns red. Ideology cannot repeal the ratios.
