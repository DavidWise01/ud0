THE DYATLOV PASS

Night of 1–2 February 1959, on the eastern shoulder of Kholat Syakhl in the northern Urals: nine experienced ski-hikers cut out of their tent into a lethal night and died. For sixty years the case was called unexplained. This panel is runnable: it holds a slab-avalanche force balance — after Gaume & Puzrin (2021) — and proves that a wind-packed slab over a buried weak layer releases once the local slope crosses a critical angle the terrain hides.

source The Dyatlov Pass incident, 1–2 Feb 1959, northern Urals; resolution: J. Gaume & A. M. Puzrin, “Mechanisms of slab avalanche release and impact in the Dyatlov Pass incident,” Communications Earth & Environment 2, 10 (2021). Room: THE WEIRD. The nine dead are treated with respect; rendered, not sensationalised.

◦ blue team · the record & the physics
1

ROOTS — the pass, Feb 1959 record

Nine members of an Ural Polytechnic ski group camped on an open slope below Kholat Syakhl. Sometime that night all nine cut the tent open from the inside and left, most without boots, into roughly −25 °C and wind. They died on the descent to the treeline; searchers found the abandoned tent on 26 February.

The Soviet inquiry closed on a phrase that outlived it: death by a “compelling natural force.” Into that gap poured sixty years of theories. The record is AMBER — history, honestly held.

2

THE EVIDENCE — why avalanche was dismissed

Two facts made an avalanche look impossible. First, the tent was not buried — it stood cut and half-standing, no debris cone. Second, some bodies had severe blunt trauma (fractured ribs, a fractured skull) without external wounds — injuries that seemed too violent for snow, yet wrong for a fall.

The measured slope at the tent read gentle, ~15° — below any textbook avalanche angle. So for decades the file said: no slab, no debris, no trigger. The evidence is AMBER; the reading of it was the error.

3

THE 2021 RESOLUTION — a delayed slab LIT

Gaume & Puzrin (2021) closed it with mechanics. Cutting the tent platform into the slope undercut a wind-loaded slab resting on a buried weak layer; hours later the slab released and slid a short way onto the sleepers — enough for blunt trauma without burial.

The key correction: the local slope under the tent was ~28–30°, not the ~15° the terrain shows — steep enough to fail. Delayed release, small slab, no debris cone. LIT.

At ~15° the weak layer holds; at the true ~28–30° the driving shear wins and the slab lets go. It slides only a short distance — enough to injure sleepers, not enough to bury the tent. The absence of a debris cone is not a missing clue; it is the small-slab signature.

6

WHY IT LOOKED IMPOSSIBLE

WALL The honest limits are real. No one measured the slab that night; the reconstruction rests on the terrain, the snowpack model and the injuries, not a caught avalanche. Three features made it look impossible at the time: a small slab (no towering debris), a delayed release hours after the tent was cut, and no burial of the tent.

Why it matters. Each of those — small, late, shallow — is exactly what the 2021 slab model predicts. The very facts once used to rule avalanche out are the fingerprints of this kind of avalanche.

7

THE GRAVEYARD — the weird theories

A yeti / menk attack. Dead. A cryptid provides no forces, no tracks, no mechanism — nothing a model can weigh against the injuries or the cut tent.

Infrasound “panic” from Karman vortices. Dead as the cause. It might explain unease, but not the ribs, the skull, or a slab that never buries — it balances no shear.

A secret Soviet weapon test / aliens. Dead. No device, no debris field, no radiation pattern that fits; folklore, not physics.

Standing: a delayed slab avalanche on a locally ~28–30° slope — peer-reviewed, and the only account that balances the forces. LIT

8

THE TAMPER — a cause with no forces

The red move: swap the slab for a fringe cause — yeti, secret weapon, “paradox.” Such a cause offers no driving and no resisting shear, so the engine has nothing to test. A real explanation balances forces; the myth cannot.

Blame the myth and the witness (5) has no force balance to check — it turns red. The fringe theories are DEAD as science; the slab avalanche is LIT.
