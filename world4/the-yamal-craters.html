THE YAMAL CRATERS

A ~20–30 m near-cylindrical hole punched through West-Siberian permafrost, ringed by ejected frozen blocks and a spike of methane at the rim. First found in 2014 near Bovanenkovo (GEC-1). Unlike its sibling ball lightning, this one is EXPLAINED: a pressure blowout. This panel is runnable — it holds the rupture threshold, a frozen cap fails when Pgas ≥ Patm + ρgh + σt, and proves the honest thing: a thin cap blows out, a very deep cap does not, and a meteor is the wrong signature. This is THE WEIRD, resolved.

◦ blue team · the hole, the numbers, the mechanism
1

ROOTS — the end of the earth record

Yamal means “the end of the earth” in Nenets. In the summer of 2014, reindeer herders and a helicopter crew reported a fresh ~20 m hole in the tundra — GEC-1 — about 30 km southeast of the Bovanenkovo gas field. It plunged tens of metres, its rim heaped with chunks of frozen ground thrown outward.

A datable, photographed, revisited landform — but the exact pre-event ground state was not instrumented, so specific field figures stay AMBER: real record, honestly held.

2

THE OBSERVATIONS — the signature

The recurring portrait across the eight GECs found on Yamal and Gydan: a deep, near-cylindrical shaft (tens of metres); radial frozen ejecta around a parapet rim; a high methane anomaly at the rim; and, within a few seasons, the shaft fills to a small lake (GEC-1: initial 50–70 m → a 3–5 m lake).

Those four facts — cylindrical, radial frozen ejecta, rim methane, later a lake — are what a mechanism must fit. They are the inputs to the engine at centre. Compiled observation: AMBER.

3

THE MECHANISM — the feed LIT numbers

Warming permafrost releases gas — methane from dissociating gas hydrates and CO₂/CH₄ from thawing cryopegs (salty unfrozen lenses). The gas accumulates under a frozen cap, heaving a mound. When internal pressure exceeds what the cap can hold, the cap ruptures and ejects the ground.

The pressure-threshold physics is LIT; the specific hydrate-dissociation pressures and field depths are AMBER (interpretation of an under-instrumented, evolving landform).

EXPLAINED, NOT OPEN — the honest line

RESOLVED This is the mirror of ball lightning. That case stays OPEN — no consensus mechanism. This one does not: the pressure-blowout model fits the shaft, the frozen ejecta, and the rim methane, and is supported across the eight known GECs. The WEIRD room must keep that difference honest — not everything strange is unsolved.

What stays AMBER. The exact pre-event pressures, cap depths, and how much comes from hydrate vs cryopeg vs warming permafrost are still being pinned down. The mechanism is LIT; the precise field numbers are AMBER — a monitoring and quantification problem, not a mystery.

LINEAGE & THE GRAVEYARD

“A meteor / a secret missile strike / a sinkhole to nowhere / an alien entrance.” Cut. The signature is an internal gas explosion: frozen blocks thrown outward, a methane spike at the rim, no shock-melt, no crater ray. What is buried is the fringe origin — DEAD.

8

THE TAMPER — “a meteor dug it”

The red move: point at the round hole and say “a meteor impact dug this crater” — ignoring the signature. The engine calls it: an impact leaves a raised shock-ejecta rim with melt and shatter and cannot strew frozen ground radially or spike the rim with methane.

Claim “a meteor impact dug the crater” and the witness (5) tests the impact signature against what is seen — frozen radial ejecta and a rim methane spike, which an impact cannot produce — so it turns red. The evidence is an internal gas blowout, not an impact.
