THE KOLA SUPERDEEP BOREHOLE

On the Kola Peninsula, Soviet drillers spent 1970–1992 boring the deepest hole ever made: 12,262 m — the deepest artificial point on Earth. The internet remembers the “Well to Hell” tape of screaming souls. That tape is a documented hoax. This panel is runnable: it computes the fraction of the planet actually drilled (~0.19% of Earth’s radius) and shows the crust was too hot — ~180 °C where a shallow-gradient line predicts ~100 °C. This is THE WEIRD: kill the legend, keep the stranger science.

◦ blue team · the hole, the readings, the lineage
1

ROOTS — the deepest hole record

Begun in 1970 on the Kola Peninsula near Murmansk, borehole SG-3 was a pure science project — not for oil, but to sample the deep continental crust of the Baltic Shield. By 1979 it passed the previous record; by 1989 it reached 12,262 m. Rising heat and plastic, flowing rock stopped it in 1992.

12.262 km is the deepest artificial point on Earth — a real, dated, documented achievement. Historical record: AMBER.

2

THE OBSERVATIONS — what the hole found

The surprises were not supernatural but geological: the temperature at the bottom was ~180 °C, far above the ~100 °C a shallow-gradient line predicted; free water was squeezed from minerals kilometres down where none should flow; the expected granite-to-basalt change (the Conrad boundary) simply was not there; and ancient microfossils turned up ~6 km deep.

Four honest findings — hotter, wetter, no basalt, fossils — are the inputs to the engine at centre. Compiled field data: AMBER.

3

LINEAGE — deep-crust geophysics theory

Kola turned a textbook picture into data. The Conrad discontinuity — long read as a granite→basalt compositional layer — was reinterpreted: at Kola it was a metamorphic / fracture boundary, not a change of rock type. The geothermal gradient steepened with depth, forcing better crustal heat-flow models.

These are advances in deep-crust geophysics — a real result, not a portal. Interpretation: AMBER, resting on the LIT measurements at centre.

red team · the graveyard, the wall, the tamper ◦
6

THE GRAVEYARD — the Well to Hell

“The drill broke into a cavern; a microphone lowered into the heat recorded the screams of the damned. The Soviets sealed the hole in terror.”

DEAD. The recording is a documented hoax — it spread from a 1989 tract and a US tabloid, and the “scream” audio was later traced to a looped horror-film soundtrack. What is buried is the legend, not the borehole: SG-3 is real, its readings are real, the cavern of the damned never existed.

7

THE WALL — the mantle is untouched

WALL Even the deepest hole ever drilled reaches ~0.19% of the way to the centre and stays entirely within the crust. The mantle — let alone the core, let alone any “hell” — is nowhere near. Depth beats a wall of heat and plastic rock, not a door.

This is a real limit, not a missing paragraph: rock at 180 °C flows and closes the bore, and drill strings cannot survive much deeper. The mystery of the deep Earth stays above the drill, read only by seismology.

8

THE TAMPER — “it reached hell”

The red move: revive the legend — “the borehole broke into the mantle and recorded the screams”. The engine calls it: the hole is a 0.19% scratch nowhere near the mantle, and the audio is a debunked hoax.

Assert “reached the mantle and recorded screams” and the witness (5) checks the fraction drilled and the hoax flag — the depth cannot reach the mantle and the tape is not evidence — so it turns red. The deep results are thermal and hydrological science, not the supernatural.
