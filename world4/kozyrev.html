◦ blue team · roots & lineage
1

ROOTS — the tragedy 1908–1983

Nikolai Aleksandrovich Kozyrev, astrophysicist at Pulkovo Observatory. In 1936 he was swept up in the Stalinist terror, arrested, and sentenced. He spent roughly a decade in the Gulag — and was freed in 1946.

He reportedly reconstructed physics from memory in the camps, having no books. The suffering is real and it is not the measure of his ideas — a wrong theory is wrong on the evidence, and a right one is right, no matter what the man endured.

2

REAL WORK — Alphonsus, 1958 LIT

On 3 November 1958, observing the lunar crater Alphonsus at Pulkovo, Kozyrev recorded a spectroscopic emission event — an apparent outgassing from the Moon, published in Sky & Telescope (1959). Gerard Kuiper disputed the plate at the time; modern consensus — and the honour naming Kozyrev a discoverer of lunar volcanism — credits it as a genuine transient lunar event.

This is sound observational astronomy. It is consistent with the later, established category of transient lunar phenomena — brief, localized brightenings and gas releases. Real, published, cited. It stands on its own.

3

LINEAGE — the honest ledger history

One life, two ledgers. Column LIT: stellar atmospheres, the Alphonsus outgassing, the stellar-energy problem of his era. Column DEAD: "causal mechanics" — time as a physical substance carrying energy, transmissible by torsion.

The torsion / "time-energy" idea was never replicated and is not accepted physics; yet it persists in fringe circles to this day. The lineage the corpus keeps is the honest one: credit the science, retire the mysticism — without erasing that it was proposed.

6

THE TURN → WHY IT FAILS DEAD

From the 1950s on, Kozyrev turned to "causal mechanics": time is not a passive coordinate but a physical substance that carries energy, with a directional "flow" transmissible through spinning gyroscopes and torsion. He reported laboratory effects to support it.

THE WALL It failed the two tests a theory must pass. (1) Unreplicated: independent labs did not reproduce the claimed torsion / time-energy effects. (2) Unfalsifiable in practice: the framework was elastic enough to absorb null results rather than be refuted by them. Not accepted physics — then or now.
7

THE GRAVEYARD

"Kozyrev proved time is energy." Cut. No independent experiment confirmed torsion / time-energy. It is DEAD-as-science — kept visible here, not erased, but not LIT.

"Gravitational contraction powers the Sun." Cut. tKH gives only ~3×107 yr of energy; the Sun is ~4.5×109 yr old. Fusion powers it.

"The Gulag years discredit his astronomy." Cut — reversed. His Alphonsus outgassing (1958) is sound, published observation. It is LIT on its evidence; the man's suffering is not the ledger.
