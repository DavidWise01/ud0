World IV · the Russian world

SONIA ★

the world that reads the descent by candlelight

KEEPER · SONIA
Sofya Semyonovna Marmeladova — she who reads Lazarus in the dark room and keeps the light between.
SONIA is the Russian and Soviet content world — the number, the novel, the machine, and the weird. She is named for Sonia Marmeladova of Dostoevsky's Crime and Punishment: the girl who reads the raising of Lazarus by a guttering candle and follows Raskolnikov all the way to Siberia. She is the moral centre that does not argue, only stays. SONIA flows naturally, and she opens the way Russia opens — with a mathematician on the shape of number itself: A. Ya. Khinchin, and the continued fraction, the descent that lives between every pair of rationals.

THE CONTINUED FRACTION
the best rational, at every depth · THE NUMBER
The Euclidean descent a₀ + 1/(a₁ + 1/(a₂ + …)) yields the single best rational at every depth — convergents, the determinant identity, the best-approximation bound, Gauss–Kuzmin, and Khinchin's constant K ≈ 2.6854520010, all computed live in THE OCHO.
A. Ya. Khinchin, Continued Fractions (Mir, 1964)
 ✓ LIT
GOBERNET
communism & socialism — the plan, computed · THE STATE
A governance instrument. The LIT centre is a runnable Leontief material-balance planner (the socialist-calculation math): a productive plan closes, an over-ambitious one returns impossible negative outputs. Around it — the roots, the lineage, the calculation problem, and an honest good/bad ledger with cited numbers on both sides.
Marx & Engels · Leontief · Kantorovich
 ✓ LIT
THE SETUN
the ternary computer — the trit, not the bit · THE MACHINE
Setun (Brusentsov, Moscow State, 1958): convert decimal to balanced ternary and back, negate by flipping every trit, add with balanced carry, and see the radix economy (base 3 beats base 2 for large N). The ternary hinge back to World III.
Brusentsov & Sobolev · Setun (1958)
 ✓ LIT
THE MARKOV PROPERTY
memoryless chains → the stationary distribution · THE NUMBER
A. A. Markov's chains: a stochastic matrix P and its stationary π (πP = π) by power iteration, seeded by Markov's own 1913 count of vowels and consonants in Pushkin's Eugene Onegin. The line to PageRank, MCMC, and hidden Markov models.
A. A. Markov · Eugene Onegin (1913)
 ✓ LIT
THE LOBACHEVSKY
the geometry where the angles fall short · THE NUMBER
Non-Euclidean geometry (Kazan, 1829): set a hyperbolic triangle's angles and watch the sum stay below π, with the defect equal to the area (Gauss–Bonnet); the Poincaré disk; infinitely many parallels. The Copernicus of geometry.
N. I. Lobachevsky (1829)
 ✓ LIT
RUSSIAN COSMISM
conquer death, fill the cosmos · THE WEIRD
Fedorov's Common Task and Tsiolkovsky, held honestly: the worldview AMBER, its literal resurrection dead-as-science, its influence on the space programme real. The runnable core: a colonisation wave filling the galaxy in ~500,000 years at 0.1c, faster-than-light barred.
Fedorov (1906) · Tsiolkovsky
 ✓ LIT
THE MORPHOLOGY OF THE TALE
the 31 functions of the wonder-tale · THE NOVEL
Propp (1928): the wonder-tale reduced to 31 functions in fixed order and 7 personae, with a valid-tale checker that accepts an in-order subsequence and rejects any reordering. Russian formalism as a computable grammar; the line to Campbell's hero's journey.
V. Propp · Morphology of the Folktale (1928)
 ✓ LIT
THE TETRIS
the Soviet game that outran the USSR · THE MACHINE
Pajitnov, Moscow, 1984: the tetrominoes classified (5 free, 7 one-sided, 19 fixed), a board that clears full rows, and the fact that offline Tetris is NP-complete (Demaine 2002). Tamper-provable by injecting a 5-cell pentomino.
Alexey Pajitnov · Tetris (1984)
 ✓ LIT
THE ONEGIN STANZA
the novel in verse, as a computable form · THE NOVEL
Pushkin's stanza (AbAbCCddEffEgg): a live rhyme-scheme validator, 6 feminine + 8 masculine lines, iambic tetrameter, and the line to Nabokov and Vikram Seth. Complements the-markov-property on the same Eugene Onegin.
A. S. Pushkin · Eugene Onegin
 ✓ LIT
THE TUNGUSKA EVENT
the largest airburst in recorded history · THE WEIRD
Siberia, 1908: the kinetic energy of a ~60 m stony asteroid at ~20 km/s → ~10–15 megatons (~1000 Hiroshimas), ~2,150 km² flattened, and the no-crater puzzle resolved by an airburst. Fringe theories held dead-as-science; the physics LIT.
Tunguska · 30 June 1908
 ✓ LIT
THE MUTUAL AID
cooperation as a factor of evolution · THE STATE
Kropotkin (1902): the evolution of cooperation vs Social Darwinism — Hamilton's rule (rb > c) and reciprocity (tit-for-tat beats always-defect). The anarchist counter to GOBERNET's Marxism; honest that mutual aid needs reciprocity or kinship.
Peter Kropotkin · Mutual Aid (1902)
 ✓ LIT
THE CHEBYSHEV INEQUALITY
any distribution, one guarantee · THE NUMBER
Chebyshev (St. Petersburg): for ANY distribution, P(|X−μ| ≥ kσ) ≤ 1/k² (25% at k=2, 11% at k=3), the tail sitting under the bound live; plus Chebyshev polynomials and Bertrand's postulate. Markov and Lyapunov were his students.
P. L. Chebyshev (1821–1894)

◆ the ternary bridge → World III · THE HALLWAY

Russia's own answer to the binary machine was balanced ternary: SETUN (Н. П. Брусенцов & С. Л. Соболев, Moscow State University, 1958) — the only series-produced ternary computer, its trit taking the three values −1, 0, +1. Ternary is the hinge where Russia and the number three become the same gesture. The ternary machinery homes in World III · THE HALLWAY (world three · ternary); its history lives here, in SONIA.
